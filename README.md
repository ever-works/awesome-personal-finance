## Budgeting Apps

- [Copilot Money](https://copilotmoney.com/) - Copilot Money is an iOS and Mac budgeting app that automatically categorizes transactions and tracks net worth. It uses rules-based automation and offers customizable dashboards. Subscription required. ([Read more](/details/copilot-money.md)) `Paid` `Budgeting` `Apple Ecosystem` `Net Worth`
- [EveryDollar](https://www.everydollar.com/) - EveryDollar is a budgeting app based on Ramsey's zero-based budgeting method. It allows manual or bank-connected expense tracking and debt payoff planning. Freemium with premium features. ([Read more](/details/everydollar.md)) `Freemium` `Budgeting` `Zero-Based Budgeting` `Ramsey`
- [Goodbudget](https://goodbudget.com/) - Goodbudget is an envelope budgeting app available on web and mobile. It supports manual transaction entry and household budget sharing without requiring bank syncing. Freemium. ([Read more](/details/goodbudget.md)) `Freemium` `Budgeting` `Envelope Method` `Manual Entry`
- [Lunch Money](https://lunchmoney.app/) - Lunch Money is a budgeting and expense-tracking app with multi-currency support and shared budgets. It imports transactions via bank connections or CSV and emphasizes manual control. Paid subscription. ([Read more](/details/lunch-money.md)) `Paid` `Budgeting` `Multi-Currency` `Shared Budgets`
- [Monarch Money](https://www.monarchmoney.com/) - Monarch Money is a web and mobile budgeting and net-worth tracking platform. It aggregates financial accounts, supports custom budgets, and provides investment performance reporting. It is a paid subscription service. ([Read more](/details/monarch-money.md)) `Paid` `Budgeting` `Net Worth` `Bank Sync`
- [Moneydance](https://moneydance.com/) - Moneydance is a desktop personal finance application for budgeting, bill payment, and investment tracking. It supports online banking and multiple currencies. One-time purchase license. ([Read more](/details/moneydance.md)) `Paid` `Desktop` `Bank Sync` `Investment Tracking`
- [Quicken Simplifi](https://www.quicken.com/simplifi) - Simplifi by Quicken is a web and mobile budgeting app that tracks spending, bills, and savings goals. It syncs with bank accounts and provides customizable watchlists. Paid subscription. ([Read more](/details/quicken-simplifi.md)) `Paid` `Budgeting` `Expense Tracking` `Bank Sync`
- [Tiller](https://tillerhq.com/) - Tiller feeds bank and credit card transactions into Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel spreadsheets. It provides automated daily updates and customizable templates for budgeting and net-worth tracking. Paid annual subscription. ([Read more](/details/tiller.md)) `Paid` `Spreadsheets` `Bank Sync` `Google Sheets`
- [YNAB](https://www.youneedabudget.com/) - YNAB is a subscription budgeting app based on the envelope method. It helps users assign every dollar a job, track spending, and manage debt. It connects to bank accounts and offers educational resources. ([Read more](/details/ynab.md)) `Paid` `Budgeting` `Bank Sync` `Envelope Method`

## Net-Worth & Investment Tracking

- [Empower](https://www.empower.com/) - Empower (formerly Personal Capital) is a free net-worth and investment tracking app. It aggregates investment accounts, analyzes portfolio fees, and offers retirement planning tools. Paid wealth management is optional. ([Read more](/details/empower.md)) `Free` `Net Worth` `Investment Tracking` `Retirement Planning`
- [Ghostfolio](https://ghostfolio.com/) - Ghostfolio is an open-source wealth management app for tracking stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrencies. It provides performance analytics and can be self-hosted or used as a managed service. MIT licensed. ([Read more](/details/ghostfolio.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Investment Tracking` `Wealth Management` `MIT`
- [Kubera](https://www.kubera.com/) - Kubera is a net-worth tracker for diverse assets including stocks, crypto, real estate, and bank accounts. It offers automatic price updates and beneficiary documentation features. Paid subscription. ([Read more](/details/kubera.md)) `Paid` `Net Worth` `Crypto` `Real Estate`
- [Sharesight](https://www.sharesight.com/) - Sharesight is an investment portfolio tracker that monitors stock performance, dividends, and tax reporting. It supports automatic trade imports from many brokers. Freemium with paid tiers. ([Read more](/details/sharesight.md)) `Freemium` `Investment Tracking` `Dividends` `Tax Reporting`

## Open-Source & Self-Hosted

- [Actual Budget](https://actualbudget.org/) - Actual Budget is a local-first, open-source budgeting app with envelope budgeting and bank syncing. It can be self-hosted or used via the official cloud service. Licensed under AGPL. ([Read more](/details/actual-budget.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Budgeting` `Envelope Method` `AGPL`
- [Firefly III](https://www.firefly-iii.org/) - Firefly III is a self-hosted personal finance manager for tracking expenses, budgets, and investments. It supports multiple currencies, import formats, and an API. Released under the MIT license. ([Read more](/details/firefly-iii.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Expense Tracking` `API` `MIT`
- [GnuCash](https://www.gnucash.org/) - GnuCash is free desktop accounting software for personal and small-business finances. It supports double-entry accounting, bank reconciliation, and investment tracking. Licensed under the GNU GPL. ([Read more](/details/gnucash.md)) `Open Source` `Desktop` `Double-Entry` `Small Business` `GPL`
- [KMyMoney](https://kmymoney.org/) - KMyMoney is an open-source personal finance manager for Linux, Windows, and macOS. It supports double-entry accounting, online banking, and investment portfolios. Licensed under GPL. ([Read more](/details/kmymoney.md)) `Open Source` `Desktop` `Double-Entry` `Online Banking` `GPL`
- [Money Manager Ex](https://moneymanagerex.org/) - Money Manager Ex is an open-source personal finance app for desktop and mobile. It tracks income, expenses, and budgets with support for multiple currencies. Licensed under GPL. ([Read more](/details/money-manager-ex.md)) `Open Source` `Desktop` `Mobile` `Budgeting` `GPL`

## Plain-Text Accounting

- [Beancount](https://beancount.github.io/) - Beancount is a command-line, plain-text accounting system that uses a simple text format to record financial transactions. It generates financial statements and supports double-entry bookkeeping. Open source. ([Read more](/details/beancount.md)) `Open Source` `Plain-Text` `Command-Line` `Double-Entry`
- [hledger](https://hledger.org/) - hledger is a command-line and web plain-text accounting tool inspired by Ledger. It supports double-entry bookkeeping, CSV import, and financial reporting. Open source. ([Read more](/details/hledger.md)) `Open Source` `Plain-Text` `Command-Line` `Double-Entry` `Haskell`
- [Ledger CLI](https://www.ledger-cli.org/) - Ledger is a command-line double-entry accounting tool that reads plain-text transaction files. It produces balance sheets, income statements, and cash-flow reports. Open source. ([Read more](/details/ledger-cli.md)) `Open Source` `Plain-Text` `Command-Line` `Double-Entry`
